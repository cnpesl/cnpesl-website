About CNP

A 21st-century engineering practice: structural design, construction and advanced diagnostics under one roof.

CNP Engineering Services Ltd is a Nigerian engineering firm based in Lagos. We design new structures, supervise their construction, and investigate existing ones with our own Lagos State licensed NDT laboratory.

Our practice is fully computerised, so we respond to clients quickly and accurately. Every project sits with an accountable engineer who can draw on specialist skills across the firm. Design, supervision and laboratory work all follow strict safety, operational and environmental standards.
