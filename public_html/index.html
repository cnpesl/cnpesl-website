Our NDT laboratory

CNP runs a fully equipped non-destructive testing laboratory, licensed by the Lagos State Government. Quality assurance managers and facility owners get clear, data-driven evidence of how their structures and materials are performing.

  • Structural investigation
  • Material appraisal
  • Accurate data gathering

Strength and integrity

Equipment

Original Schmidt hammers, MATEST ultrasonic pulse velocity tester

Purpose

Compressive strength measurement and internal uniformity assessment, detecting voids and deep-seated anomalies

Sub-surface and rebar

Equipment

Profoscope, LG-G100 rebar detector

Purpose

Locating embedded reinforcement, measuring concrete cover and mapping rebar grids

Dimensional and flaw

Equipment

HC-F600 crack tester, ultrasonic thickness gauge, 100 m laser tape, precision calipers

Purpose

Micro-flaw detection, thickness measurement where only one face is accessible, and exact dimensional records
