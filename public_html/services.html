Structural design

Analysis and design of reinforced concrete and steel structures, from homes to multi-storey commercial, hospitality and institutional buildings. We work to BS 8110, Eurocode and BS 5950, and deliver calculations and construction drawings that contractors can build from.

Civil infrastructure

Design, planning and front-end services for roads, drainage, bridges, culverts and intersections, and for water resources and transportation works.

Structural investigation and appraisal

Condition surveys and integrity assessments of existing buildings, including fire-damaged structures, ageing frames and buildings planned for extension or change of use. We combine site inspection, NDT and structural analysis, and give clear recommendations for repair or strengthening.

Construction supervision and project management

Supervision of structural works on site so that what is built matches the design, with a project team that keeps clear lines of responsibility with the client and other consultants.

Our NDT laboratory

CNP runs a fully equipped non-destructive testing laboratory, licensed by the Lagos State Government. Quality assurance managers and facility owners get clear, data-driven evidence of how their structures and materials are performing.

  • Structural investigation
  • Material appraisal
  • Accurate data gathering

Strength and integrity

Equipment

Original Schmidt hammers, MATEST ultrasonic pulse velocity tester

Purpose

Compressive strength measurement and internal uniformity assessment, detecting voids and deep-seated anomalies

Sub-surface and rebar

Equipment

Profoscope, LG-G100 rebar detector

Purpose

Locating embedded reinforcement, measuring concrete cover and mapping rebar grids

Dimensional and flaw

Equipment

HC-F600 crack tester, ultrasonic thickness gauge, 100 m laser tape, precision calipers

Purpose

Micro-flaw detection, thickness measurement where only one face is accessible, and exact dimensional records
